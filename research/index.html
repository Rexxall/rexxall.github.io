When does a person need help, and when should a machine step in?

I trained in medicine. I build tools around this question, and I want to study it where it starts: in the brain.

The brain changing state

What I want to study. Going from awake to asleep takes the brain a few minutes. I want to see that switch as it happens, and learn when the brain is ready for something and when it is not.

A year on the wards

Internal medicine, surgery, neurology, psychiatry, emergency, night shifts. On rounds I learned to put symptoms and medicines on one timeline before guessing a cause. In neurology I watched EEG and imaging steer a diagnosis; in psychiatry, sleep trouble came with many conditions.

Reading a claim, not repeating it

For a treatment question I framed the PICO, searched PubMed, judged allocation, blinding and follow-up, and worked the result down to a number a doctor can use.

Learning the signal

Since October 2026 I have been learning to read sleep EEG on public recordings, starting from the stages and the spectrum.

Tools that choose their moment

What I already build. Eight of my plugins are in Obsidian’s community directory. Each one ended up answering the same question in a small way.

Rules the tools follow

  1. Let the reader answer first. Noesis asks you to explain a passage before it shows anything.
  2. Short of facts? Count on the safe side. Japan Work Secretary counts a shift in full until you confirm the break, the way a ward plans for the worse case.
  3. Not sure? Don’t guess. Git Note History links a renamed note to its past only when the text matches closely.
  4. You changed it? Step back. Before writing, a plugin checks the note again and stops if it moved.

How I work

Check before building

Before I commit to a question I search for who answered it already, and ask several models to argue against me. When a search showed one of my questions had been answered in 2021, I dropped it and started over.

Ship, then keep shipping

I decide what to build, use every version, find what is off and choose the fix. AI writes most of the code, and I am learning to program along the way. The eight plugins have had 103 releases since July 2026.
